# numr v0.9.0

## Choose your decimal precision

The web and desktop apps now have a decimal places control in the footer. Choose 0–28 places, or leave it on **Auto** for the same formatting as before. The setting is saved locally, separately from your document.

Changing precision updates results and totals immediately. It only changes how values are displayed: calculations keep their full precision, and your text, undo history, and unsaved changes stay intact.

Embedded calculators get the same option through the `decimal-places` attribute and `decimalPlaces` property. Each calculator has its own setting; remove the attribute or set the property to `undefined` to return to Auto.

## Kazakhstan tenge

Thanks to [@Gallardo994](https://github.com/Gallardo994) for Kazakhstan tenge support in [core PR #20](https://github.com/nasedkinpv/numr/pull/20): `KZT`, `tenge`, and `₸`. Tenge participates in currency calculations, conversions, and grouped totals alongside the existing currencies.

## Calculation fixes

- Comments and discarded prose no longer introduce accidental answer or total references.
- ANS references follow the parsed expression, including nested references and assignment names, rather than matching words in the input.
- Display-only total lines do not feed back into later totals. Re-evaluating a document keeps totals stable.
- Failed continuations and preview evaluation preserve the previous successful answer consistently.
